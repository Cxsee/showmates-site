Privacy Policy

Effective 1 October 2026 · updated 17 October 2026 · version 2026-10

In short. We only collect what the app needs to work: your account, what you watch and what you share with friends. We don't sell your data, we don't show ads and we don't build advertising profiles. For personalized recommendations the app builds a profile of your taste, which you can turn off at any time (details). You can export your data or delete your account at any time from the app.

1. Who is responsible for your data

Andrea Caselli, Italy (data controller). Contact for any privacy request: support@showmates.app.

This policy covers the Showmates app for Android and the showmates.app website.

2. What we collect

Account

  • Email address and password. The password is handled by the sign-in service (Supabase Auth) and stored only in hashed form: we never see it.
  • If you sign in with Google: your Google email address, name and profile picture.
  • Username, app language, public or private profile choice, date and version of the accepted terms.

Profile (optional)

  • Profile photo and bio, if you choose to add them.

What you do in the app

  • Shows, anime and movies you follow, episodes and movies watched with their date, ratings, reviews, lists and the people (actors, directors…) you follow.
  • Social activity: people you follow and who follow you, follow requests, blocked users, reactions, watch pacts and their notes, group picks ("Pick together").
  • Comments and reviews you write on shows, movies and episodes (reviews are visible to your followers, or to everyone with a public profile), the likes you give to comments and lists, and your reactions and replies to friends' activity in the feed (replies are visible to whoever can see the activity: its author and their followers).
  • Games: challenges, tournaments, quizzes, the daily challenge, scores and messages exchanged in challenges.
  • The 👍/👎 votes you give to content generated by the app and which content you've already seen, so we don't show it again.
  • Reports you send or that concern your content.
  • Which features you use and when (for example: opening the app, adding a show, finishing a challenge, sharing your Wrapped), linked to your account, to understand what to improve, only if you turn them on in Profile → Settings → "Usage statistics" (they're off for new sign-ups). Other users don't see them and we don't hand them to anyone.

Importing your history (optional)

If you import your history from Netflix (or TV Time, Trakt, MyAnimeList, Letterboxd, IMDb), the file is read on your phone. Only the titles reach our servers, to recognize them; the recognized ones are added to your history. AniList is imported with your username: the app asks AniList for your public list directly, without going through our servers.

So you can use the app offline, your phone keeps a copy of your library, upcoming episodes, calendar and stats. The copy is deleted when you sign out.

Only on your phone

The streaming services you subscribe to (Netflix, Prime Video…) are saved only on your phone, not in your account: we send them to our servers only while computing your recommendations, to prefer titles you can watch, and we don't keep them. That's why they aren't in your data export and they're cleared when you sign out or change phone.

Device data

  • A push notification identifier (Firebase Cloud Messaging), if you allow notifications.
  • Your phone's time zone, to count days, stats and the daily challenge in your local time. It isn't visible to other users.
  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) when the app closes because of an error or runs into one it can handle: phone model, Android and app version, technical details of the error and an installation identifier. They don't contain your name or email. You can turn them off in Profile → Settings → "Send error reports".
  • The country used for streaming services, taken from your phone's settings.
  • Technical server logs (for example IP address and time of requests), kept by our providers for security.

We don't collect your location, contacts, microphone or any other phone data. Profile photos are uploaded without metadata (no location or phone model).

3. What other users can see

  • Your username and photo are visible to other users. Your bio is visible to everyone with a public profile; with a private profile, only to your followers.
  • With a public profile anyone using the app can see what you watch, your reviews and your stats. With a private profile only the people you approve can.
  • Comments are visible to everyone who opens that title or episode. With a public profile they show your username and photo; with a private profile, people who don't follow you see them without your name or photo. A deleted comment no longer shows who wrote it.
  • Public lists are visible to everyone; with a private profile, only to your followers. "Link only" lists are visible only to people who have the link.
  • People in a watch pact or a challenge with you see the progress the game needs. Leaderboards show username and score.
  • If personalized recommendations are on, a title you loved may appear in your followers' recommendations with your username ("… loved it"). Only your followers see it.
  • When you ask to delete your account, during the 14-day wait your profile, comments and lists stay visible as before; then they disappear.
  • You can block anyone: blocked people no longer see your profile and can't contact you.

Personalized recommendations

To suggest what to watch, the app builds a profile of your taste. Under the GDPR this is profiling, so here is how it works:

  • What it uses: the titles in your library and their status, ratings and reviews, what you finished or dropped, your 👍/👎 on recommendations, your viewing habits (at what times and on which days you watch, how many episodes in a row, in your phone's time zone), the streaming services you picked and the titles loved by the people you follow.
  • The "people who loved X also loved Y" model: we regularly train a statistical model on the libraries of all users who have recommendations on. In the model each person is just a series of numbers, with no name or email.
  • What it's for: only choosing and ranking recommendations inside the app. No ads, and no decisions with legal or similarly significant effects on you. No other user sees your taste profile, and it's included in your data export.
  • Legal basis: legitimate interest in giving you useful suggestions. It's on by default.
  • How to object: Profile → Settings → "Personalized recommendations". Turning them off deletes your taste profile, the recommendations computed for you and your 👍/👎 on them, removes you from the model, and you'll only see trending and generic suggestions. You can also write to the address in section 12.

5. Artificial intelligence

The recap ("Previously on…") and some quiz questions are written by an AI model (Groq) from public texts: TVmaze episode summaries and Wikipedia movie plots. We send the AI only the title and these public texts, never personal data. AI-written content may contain mistakes: you can flag it with 👎.

7. How long we keep it

  • As long as you have an account. When you ask to delete it, your profile photo goes away right now and after 14 days (time to change your mind) we delete the account and all related data.
  • Data about which features you use is kept for 6 months.
  • Every night we make an encrypted backup copy of the database, kept for 30 days: after your account is deleted your data may remain in these copies for up to 30 days, then it's gone. We use them only to restore the service after a failure.
  • Technical copies in our providers' backup systems may remain for a limited time, then they are overwritten.
  • We keep moderation reports, with the copy of the reported content, for as long as they're needed to handle the report, any appeals and repeat offenders, and to defend ourselves against legal claims. Once the account is deleted they're no longer linked to you.
  • Crash reports are kept by Firebase for 90 days.

8. Your rights

You can ask us at any time to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its processing, or to receive it in a readable format (portability). Many of these you can do directly in the app:

  • Edit your name, photo and bio: in your Profile. Public or private profile: Profile → Settings (gear icon).
  • Export your data: Profile → Settings → "Export my data".
  • Delete your account: Profile → Settings → at the bottom, "Delete account and data", or as explained on the Delete account page.

For anything else write to support@showmates.app: we reply within 30 days. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority, in Italy the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (garanteprivacy.it).

9. Minimum age

Showmates is meant for people aged 16 and over. If we find out that an account belongs to someone younger, we delete it.

10. Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS). In the database each person can only read the data they're entitled to, and passwords are handled by the sign-in service in hashed form. No system is 100% secure: if a breach affecting you ever happens, we'll notify you as the law requires.

11. Changes

If we change this policy we update the date at the top. If the changes are significant we'll tell you in the app.

13. Website waiting list

Before launch you can leave your email on showmates.app. We only store the email, your language, which phone you have (Android or iPhone), whether you want to try the app early, which link brought you (for example TikTok) and, if you used a friend's invite link, their username: when you sign up in the app with the same email you're connected right away. We use it only to tell you about the launch and, if you asked, to invite you to try it early. Legal basis: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time by writing to support@showmates.app. The data is stored on Supabase (EU) and deleted at the latest 6 months after the app launches.
