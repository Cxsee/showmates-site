Community rules

Showmates · effective October 16, 2026

Showmates is for tracking shows, anime and movies and talking about them with friends. Comments, lists, reviews, pact notes and profiles are seen by other people, so these rules apply on top of the Terms of Use.

The rules

  • Respect. Criticize shows, not people. Insults, threats, harassment, discrimination, hate speech, spam and advertising are not allowed.
  • Spoilers. If you reveal what happens, mark your comment as a spoiler. Comments on an episode are only visible to people who have watched it.
  • Forbidden content. Sexually explicit content, graphic violence, self-harm, illegal activities, other people's personal data, piracy links and impersonation.
  • Age. You must be at least 16 to sign up.

Child safety: zero tolerance

Showmates prohibits any content or behavior that sexually exploits, abuses or endangers children. This includes child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, the sexualization of minors and sextortion.

  • Reported content is reviewed as a priority and removed as soon as it is confirmed.
  • The accounts involved are permanently suspended.
  • Where the law requires it, we report cases to the competent authorities and to the relevant national and international organizations (for example NCMEC).

How to report

  • In the app: every profile, comment and list has Report. Choose the reason (including Child safety). The reported person won't know who reported them, and you can also block them.
  • By email: support@showmates.app.
  • If a child is in immediate danger, call your local emergency number or the police.

Consequences

Content that breaks the rules is removed. In serious or repeated cases the account is suspended.

Child safety contact

For reports and questions about child safety: support@showmates.app.
