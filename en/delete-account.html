Delete your Showmates account

Showmates

In the app

  1. Open Showmates, go to your Profile and tap the Settings gear icon at the top.
  2. Scroll to the bottom, to the Danger zone, and tap Delete account and data.
  3. Choose how:
    • Delete account: your profile photo goes away right now; the account and all your data are deleted after 14 days. Until then your profile, comments and lists stay visible as before.
    • Delete now: after a second confirmation we delete the account and all your data right away, and they can't be recovered. If you haven't signed in recently, we first ask for a code that we send you by email.

During those 14 days you can change your mind: a notice with Cancel deletion appears in your Profile. After 14 days the deletion is final.

Before deleting your account you can download your data: Profile → Settings → Export my data.

Without the app

If you no longer have the app or can't sign in, write to support@showmates.app from your account's email address, with the subject "Account deletion" and your username. We check that the request comes from you, delete the account within 30 days (usually much sooner) and confirm by email when it's done.

What gets deleted

  • Account: email, password, username, Google sign-in.
  • Profile: photo, bio, settings.
  • History: shows, anime and movies you follow, episodes and movies watched, ratings, reviews, lists, people (actors, directors…) you follow.
  • Social activity: followers and people you follow, requests, blocks, reactions, comments, likes, replies in the feed, watch pacts and notes.
  • Personalized recommendations: taste profile, computed recommendations and your 👍/👎.
  • Games: challenges, tournaments, quizzes, scores and messages.
  • Your votes on content and the history of content you've already seen.
  • Push notification identifiers.

The streaming services you picked are stored only on your phone and are cleared when you sign out.

What may remain, and for how long

  • Our encrypted nightly backup copy of the database: your data may remain there for up to 30 days after deletion, then it's gone.
  • Technical copies in our providers' backups, for a limited time, until they are overwritten.
  • Moderation reports: they may keep a copy of the reported content, no longer linked to the account, for as long as needed to handle abuse, appeals and legal claims.
  • App crash reports: they contain no name or email, and Firebase deletes them after 90 days.

Deleting only some data

You can remove your photo, bio, reviews, followed titles and watched episodes from the app at any time. You can also download your data before deleting your account: Profile → Settings → Export my data. For any other request write to support@showmates.app.
